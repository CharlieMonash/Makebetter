StAMPS marking rubric

Each scenario gives up to 7 points in five independently scored domains. The three management domains must incorporate relevant medical and rural contextual factors.
Management — Part 1Answer exactly the first question asked. Prioritise the immediate problem, safe management and StAMPSville-specific capability / constraints.0–7
Management — Part 2Advance the case as new information appears. Re-rank the differential, interpret results and change management decisively.0–7
Management — Part 3Complete the episode: definitive care, escalation / retrieval where needed, discharge / prevention / follow-up and continuity.0–7
Problem definition & systematic approachDefine what the problem is before listing actions. Be structured without sounding formulaic. Prioritise dangerous and likely diagnoses, then use findings to narrow them.0–7
Communication & professionalismSpeak clearly as the senior doctor, use age-appropriate and family-centred communication, cultural safety, confidentiality, consent and collegial leadership.0–7

Self-marking rule

Do not reward yourself for “mentioning” a concept. Give yourself the mark only if your spoken answer made the action operational: who, what, when, where, and why in StAMPSville.

Example: “I would consider retrieval” is weak. “Given persistent shock despite first-line resuscitation and a 600 km transfer, I would call aeromedical retrieval now, involve the receiving paediatric service, continue stabilisation locally and prepare for a prolonged wait if the aircraft is unavailable” demonstrates an actual plan.

What to learn from 2024–2025

The papers span acute illness, neonates, development, chronic care, mental health, prevention, remote support and procedural / safety issues. That argues against studying “likely diagnoses” only. Build flexible frameworks and broad curriculum coverage.

Practice conversion

For every old question or 100 Cases chapter, rewrite it into three prompts: (1) initial assessment / differential, (2) investigation or deterioration, (3) definitive management / follow-up / rural complication. Then score all five rubric domains.
